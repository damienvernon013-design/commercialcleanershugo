Nightly Commercial Cleaning in Hugo, MN

Documented overnight cleaning for offices, retail, and light industrial facilities across Hugo and the East Metro.

What nightly commercial cleaning actually covers

Nightly commercial cleaning is the most common service we provide, and the one with the most variation in what gets done. Some crews clean the restrooms and empty the trash and call it a night. We work from a written scope that specifies every task, every frequency, and every standard — so there's nothing to argue about when something gets missed.

A standard nightly program at a 5,000-square-foot Hugo office building typically covers: workstation surface wipe-down, keyboard and phone sanitation, waste receptacle emptying and liner replacement, vacuuming of carpeted areas, hard-floor dust mopping and damp mopping, restroom disinfection and supply replenishment, entry and lobby cleaning, and breakroom wipe-down including sink and counters. Kitchen appliance surfaces are included on a rotating schedule. Windows are wiped at the interior perimeter weekly.

What happens during a scope walkthrough

Before we quote, we walk the facility with whoever manages it. We note floor types, restroom count, kitchen setup, any areas with special requirements (server rooms, labs, secure areas), and access logistics. That walkthrough produces a written scope document. The quote is based on the scope, not on square footage alone. You know exactly what you're buying.
