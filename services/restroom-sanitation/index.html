Restroom Sanitation Programs in Hugo, MN

Structured restroom programs with documented touchpoint disinfection, product replenishment, and drain maintenance.

Why restrooms need a program, not just a cleaning

Restroom complaints are the single most common reason a facility manager changes cleaning companies. The problem isn't usually that the restroom wasn't cleaned — it's that the cleaning was inconsistent, supplies ran out mid-week, or the drain odor returned three days after the last visit. A structured restroom program addresses all three.

What a restroom sanitation program includes

Our standard restroom program covers the following on each service visit:

  • Toilet and urinal disinfection — bowl, seat, exterior, and flush handle
  • Sink, faucet, and countertop disinfection
  • Mirror cleaning
  • Door handle, push plate, and light switch disinfection
  • Waste receptacle emptying and liner replacement
  • Soap, paper towel, and toilet paper replenishment
  • Floor disinfection and grout line scrubbing (weekly)
  • Drain treatment (per schedule)
  • Partition wipe-down

Supply replenishment

We track usage and flag supply levels on each visit. If paper products or soap are running lower than expected, you'll know before the restroom is empty. We don't manage your supply purchasing, but we will tell you what to order and how much.

Odor management

Drain odor in restrooms has one of two causes: biofilm buildup in the trap, or inadequate ventilation. We address the drain side with enzymatic treatment on a scheduled basis. Ventilation is a facilities issue, but we flag it when we see it.

Coverage areas

Restroom sanitation programs are available across the full Hugo service area. The program is included as part of most nightly cleaning contracts and can be scheduled as a standalone service for facilities with high restroom traffic.
