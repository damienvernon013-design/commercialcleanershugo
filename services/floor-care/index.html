Commercial floor care for VCT, LVT, polished concrete, carpet, and tile across Hugo and the East Metro.

Commercial floor care in a Minnesota climate

Hugo businesses deal with road salt from October through April. Salt residue degrades finish faster than any other single factor in a Minnesota commercial building — it etches VCT, leaves white hazing on polished concrete, and traps in carpet pile until it becomes abrasive. A floor care program that ignores seasonal conditions will cost more in refinishing over three years than one that accounts for them upfront.

Our floor care programs are built around what the floor type actually needs, not what the same crew applies to everything. We assess floor type, traffic volume, finish history, and seasonal conditions before recommending a program.

Strip and wax: when it's needed and when it's not

Strip and wax is the most disruptive floor service — the facility needs to be clear, the process takes hours, and the fumes require ventilation. Most facilities don't need a full strip more than once or twice a year. Between strips, scrub-and-recoat extends the life of the finish and keeps the floor looking maintained without the downtime. We'll tell you which one you actually need.
