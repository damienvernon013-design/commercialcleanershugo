Our Commercial Cleaning Services in Hugo, MN

22 years serving East Metro businesses with documented, scope-driven cleaning programs.

Services we provide

Hugo Commercial Cleaners operates on written scope documents. Before any work starts, we walk your facility, document what gets cleaned, how often, and at what standard. That document is the contract. The three core services below are the building blocks of most programs — most clients use a combination of all three.

Nightly Commercial Cleaning

Workstations, restrooms, kitchens, and common areas on a documented nightly or multi-weekly schedule. The core service for most Hugo businesses.

Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance

Strip and wax, scrub and recoat, burnishing, carpet extraction, and polished concrete maintenance. Minnesota winters demand more than a mop.

Restroom Sanitation Programs

Structured restroom cleaning with touchpoint disinfection, supply replenishment, and drain maintenance on a documented schedule.

How programs are built

We don't quote from a price sheet. We walk the facility, note every room type, floor type, and traffic pattern, and produce a written scope before any number is discussed. That scope is what you buy. Most programs start with a walkthrough call at (866) 958-8773.
