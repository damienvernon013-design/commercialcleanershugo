## Town-specific facts (≥3 per town)

**Forest Lake**
1. Commercial corridor runs along Highway 61 with auto-service, medical, and light-industrial buildings
2. Forest Lake Area Schools central office and district administrative buildings drive professional cleaning demand
3. Heavy road-salt application at Highway 61 / I-35 convergence — salt hazing on VCT runs through April

**White Bear Lake**
1. Downtown 4th Street district: boutique retail, professional offices, restaurants — different cleaning profile than big-box south of town
2. 3M campus proximity draws supplier/vendor offices exceeding population demand
3. Above-average number of single-story medical clinic buildings along Highway 61

**Lino Lakes**
1. Significant commercial development along Hodgson Road and Century Avenue — newer buildings with polished concrete (not VCT)
2. I-35W location makes it a distribution node; second/third-shift operations affect cleaning window scheduling
3. Ramsey/Anoka County border produces unusual zoning mix including secured storage facilities

**Centerville**
1. Commercial footprint on Main Street and County Road 14 — dental practices, small professional offices, light retail
2. City sits at Ramsey County's northern edge; many facilities carry multiple postal addresses (Centerville, Circle Pines, Blaine)
3. Cold air drainage from surrounding terrain extends frost season — de-icing residue runs November through late March

**Grant**
1. Township (not a city) with equestrian facilities and hobby farms with associated office buildings
2. St. Croix River valley edge includes residential developments with association management offices and clubhouses
3. Owner-operated commercial buildings on Manning Avenue and Stillwater Boulevard — value reliability over price

**Dellwood**
1. Small residential township; facilities near Dellwood/White Bear Lake line often carry White Bear Lake addresses
2. Private club and association facilities along White Bear Lake shore with seasonal event cleaning requirements
3. Lake proximity causes elevated humidity in spring and fall — affects grout and tile maintenance needs

**Mahtomedi**
1. Commercial core along Stillwater Road: financial services, insurance agencies, medical specialists in single-story buildings
2. School district administrative offices on Wildwood Road among the larger non-retail facilities
3. Located on Highway 244 commuter route — high morning/evening lobby and entry traffic increases cleaning demand relative to building size

**Stillwater**
1. Historic downtown Main Street with Victorian-era commercial buildings — multi-story brick, older floor finishes, tight back-of-house access
2. Liberty on the Lake and Croix Landing developments have modern office with polished concrete and LVT (different profile from older stock)
3. Washington County seat — county offices and courthouse-adjacent buildings require access logs and background-checked crew
