Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Hugo, MN

What our coverage means for your facility and how to verify it.

General liability insurance

Hugo Commercial Cleaners carries general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations. This coverage protects your facility against property damage caused by our crew during service visits. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote and can be provided to your building owner or property manager on request.

Workers' compensation

All crew members working in your facility are covered by workers' compensation insurance. This matters for two reasons: it protects the workers, and it protects you. A cleaning company without workers' comp exposes the building owner to liability if a crew member is injured on-site. We carry it. We can confirm it in writing.

Bonding

We are bonded. Bonding provides protection in the event of theft by a crew member. It's a relatively low-cost safeguard and one that any cleaning company working in occupied commercial facilities should carry. If your facility has after-hours access requirements or if crew members will work in secure areas, ask any cleaning company you're evaluating whether they're bonded before the conversation goes further.

22 years in business

A cleaning company that has operated for 22 years has resolved most of the problems that put younger companies out of business — insurance gaps, crew turnover, contract disputes, scheduling failures. We've been through all of it. The business is still here because we fixed those problems rather than absorbing them into a revolving door of clients. That history is our strongest credential, and we don't let a page go by without saying it: 22 years in business, insured, bonded, and still answering our own phone.
