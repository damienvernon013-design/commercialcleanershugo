Commercial Cleaning Pricing in Hugo, MN

What cleaning costs in the East Metro and why the number without a scope is meaningless.

How commercial cleaning is priced

Commercial cleaning in Hugo and the East Metro is priced by a combination of square footage, facility type, service frequency, floor mix, and restroom count. A 4,000-square-foot office building with three restrooms and nightly service costs more than a 4,000-square-foot warehouse with one restroom serviced twice a week. Square footage alone is a rough guess, not a quote.

We don't publish fixed rates because they mislead. A rate that looks low often assumes minimal scope — one restroom, hard floors only, light traffic. A rate that looks high may include tasks your facility doesn't need. The only number worth discussing is the one based on a written scope for your specific building.

What drives cost up

  • Higher service frequency (nightly versus twice weekly)
  • More restrooms relative to square footage
  • Carpet-heavy floor mix (more time than hard floors)
  • Kitchen or breakroom with food prep
  • Medical or food-service compliance requirements
  • After-hours access requirements with security protocols

What drives cost down

  • Lower service frequency
  • Hard floors throughout (faster to maintain than carpet)
  • Single-story, open floor plan with minimal partitioned rooms
  • Predictable access (key or code, no escort required)
  • Multi-year contract commitment
