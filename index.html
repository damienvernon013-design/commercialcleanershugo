Commercial Cleaning Services in Hugo, MN

Hugo sits where Washington County's suburban growth meets its rural edges — a mix of light industrial properties along Highway 61, office parks near downtown, and retail serving the surrounding townships. Most of the businesses here have the same problem: a cleaning company that covered the basics when the account was new and coasted after that. Hugo Commercial Cleaners works on written scope documents, which means the crew knows exactly what gets done on every visit.

Nightly Commercial Cleaning

Workstations, common areas, restrooms, entry glass, and waste removal on your schedule — nightly, three times a week, or tailored to your occupancy.

Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance

Scrubbing, buffing, burnishing, strip-and-wax, and carpet extraction. Hard-surface floors in Minnesota winters need more than a mop.

Restroom Sanitation Programs

Structured restroom programs with product replenishment, touchpoint disinfection, and drain maintenance on a documented schedule.

Why the Written Scope Matters

Most cleaning companies give you a price and a handshake. The problem is that "nightly cleaning" means something different to every crew and every supervisor. We document what gets cleaned, how often, and at what standard before the first visit. That document is what your 30-day inspection is measured against, and it's what we hold ourselves to when staff changes happen on our end.
