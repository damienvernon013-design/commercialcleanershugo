What to Expect: Your First 30 Days with Hugo Commercial Cleaners

From first call to first 30-day review — the process from start to stable.

The process from inquiry to ongoing service

1

Facility walkthrough

We walk every area we'll clean — offices, restrooms, kitchen, storage rooms, entry areas. We note floor types, furniture density, traffic patterns, and any access or security requirements. This takes 20–40 minutes for most facilities.

2

Written scope of work

The walkthrough produces a scope document: every task, every frequency, every standard. This document is attached to the contract. It tells the crew exactly what gets done and tells you exactly what to measure us against.

3

Crew assignment

We assign a crew based on facility type, shift requirements, and proximity. We introduce the lead to the facility contact before the first service date.

4

First clean

The first service visit runs long — we're learning the space. Expect the crew to take more time than subsequent visits as they familiarize themselves with the scope and the facility layout.

5

Day-15 check-in

At two weeks we call to ask what's working and what isn't. Small issues caught here don't become month-two complaints.

6

30-day scope review

At 30 days we walk the facility again and compare what we're delivering against the scope document. If anything needs adjusting — task frequency, product choice, access logistics — we adjust it before month two.

Response time commitment

During business hours (Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm), we answer at (866) 958-8773. After hours, voicemail and the contact form both get a next-business-day response. We don't promise 24/7 availability because we can't back that up with the staffing it requires. We do promise that a real person answers during business hours and responds to messages the next business day.

When something goes wrong

It will, at some point. The question is what happens next. Our protocol: call or email, we acknowledge within one business day, we identify whether the issue was crew, scope, or access, and we fix it within one service cycle. If the same issue recurs, we escalate to a supervisor walkthrough.
