Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance in Grant, MN

Commercial floor care for Grant offices and facilities — VCT, LVT, carpet, polished concrete.

Floor care in Grant

Commercial floor care in Grant follows the same pattern as the broader East Metro: Minnesota winter conditions accelerate finish degradation, and facilities that don't address salt residue and moisture tracking in November are dealing with a much bigger problem by March.

A typical Grant floor care scenario

A 5,000-square-foot equestrian facility office and event space near Manning Avenue in Grant Township — LVT through the main event room, carpet in the office wing, and sealed concrete in the utility corridor. The facility hosts events on weekend evenings, so deep floor cleaning must happen Monday mornings after weekend events. The LVT shows gouging from chair legs and event equipment; the carpet needs extraction after each event season.

Services available in Grant

  • VCT strip and wax, scrub and recoat, burnishing
  • LVT/LVP scrub and recoat, low-speed maintenance
  • Polished concrete guard application and diamond-pad maintenance
  • Carpet hot-water extraction and interim encapsulation
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile scrub, grout cleaning, and sealing
  • Hardwood screening and recoating (surface work, not sanding)
