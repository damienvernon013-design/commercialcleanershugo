Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance in Centerville, MN

Commercial floor care for Centerville offices and facilities — VCT, LVT, carpet, polished concrete.

Floor care in Centerville

Commercial floor care in Centerville follows the same pattern as the broader East Metro: Minnesota winter conditions accelerate finish degradation, and facilities that don't address salt residue and moisture tracking in November are dealing with a much bigger problem by March.

A typical Centerville floor care scenario

A 7,500-square-foot Centerville retail building near the County Road 14 and Main Street intersection — VCT throughout the sales floor, ceramic tile at the entry, and carpet in the back storage office. The VCT was stripped and waxed before the current tenant moved in two years ago and has had no maintenance since. The finish is dull, there's a visible traffic lane worn through the center of the floor, and the entry tile has hard-water deposits from the floor mat cleaning station.

Services available in Centerville

  • VCT strip and wax, scrub and recoat, burnishing
  • LVT/LVP scrub and recoat, low-speed maintenance
  • Polished concrete guard application and diamond-pad maintenance
  • Carpet hot-water extraction and interim encapsulation
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile scrub, grout cleaning, and sealing
  • Hardwood screening and recoating (surface work, not sanding)
