Nightly commercial cleaning in Centerville

Centerville is part of our regular service route — approximately 12 minutes west-southwest of Hugo. Nightly cleaning accounts for the majority of our Centerville work, covering offices, medical facilities, and mixed-use commercial buildings.

A typical Centerville cleaning scenario

A 3,200-square-foot dental practice on County Road 14 in Centerville — four operatories, a sterilization room, a waiting room, two restrooms, and a staff break room. Nightly cleaning five days a week with documented surface protocols: no cross-contamination between operatories, separate microfiber sets for clinical and non-clinical areas, dated disposal logs on request. The practice administrator needs a written record of what was cleaned and when.

What the scope covers

Every nightly program is documented. Standard nightly scope for a Centerville office building includes workstation surface cleaning, restroom disinfection, kitchen and breakroom wipe-down, waste removal, vacuuming or hard-floor maintenance, and entry glass cleaning. Tasks outside this list go in the scope document before the contract starts.

Schedule options

We serve Centerville facilities on nightly (5x/week), three-times-weekly, and twice-weekly schedules. The right frequency depends on occupancy, restroom count, and how much food-service activity happens in the building. We recommend a frequency during the scope walkthrough, not before it.
