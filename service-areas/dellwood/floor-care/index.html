Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance in Dellwood, MN

Commercial floor care for Dellwood offices and facilities — VCT, LVT, carpet, polished concrete.

Floor care in Dellwood

Commercial floor care in Dellwood follows the same pattern as the broader East Metro: Minnesota winter conditions accelerate finish degradation, and facilities that don't address salt residue and moisture tracking in November are dealing with a much bigger problem by March.

A typical Dellwood floor care scenario

A Dellwood shoreline facility with hardwood floors in the event hall and tile in the entry and kitchen areas. The hardwood has surface scratches from event chair use and needs professional screening and recoating — not a full sand, but more than a standard buff. The tile entry shows mineral deposits from the high-humidity months near the lake. Seasonal timing matters: the work should happen in early May before the event season starts.

Services available in Dellwood

  • VCT strip and wax, scrub and recoat, burnishing
  • LVT/LVP scrub and recoat, low-speed maintenance
  • Polished concrete guard application and diamond-pad maintenance
  • Carpet hot-water extraction and interim encapsulation
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile scrub, grout cleaning, and sealing
  • Hardwood screening and recoating (surface work, not sanding)
