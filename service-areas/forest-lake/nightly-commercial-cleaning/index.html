Nightly commercial cleaning in Forest Lake

Forest Lake is part of our regular service route — approximately 10 minutes north of Hugo via Highway 61. Nightly cleaning accounts for the majority of our Forest Lake work, covering offices, medical facilities, and mixed-use commercial buildings.

A typical Forest Lake cleaning scenario

A 6,200-square-foot multi-suite professional building on 18th Avenue SW in Forest Lake — two dental offices and a physical therapy practice on the ground floor, a financial planning firm on the second. Four restrooms, a shared waiting room on each floor, and a common-area coffee station. Nightly cleaning five days a week, with the dental suites requiring documented surface wipe-down schedules that meet the practice's infection-control protocols.

What the scope covers

Every nightly program is documented. Standard nightly scope for a Forest Lake office building includes workstation surface cleaning, restroom disinfection, kitchen and breakroom wipe-down, waste removal, vacuuming or hard-floor maintenance, and entry glass cleaning. Tasks outside this list go in the scope document before the contract starts.

Schedule options

We serve Forest Lake facilities on nightly (5x/week), three-times-weekly, and twice-weekly schedules. The right frequency depends on occupancy, restroom count, and how much food-service activity happens in the building. We recommend a frequency during the scope walkthrough, not before it.
