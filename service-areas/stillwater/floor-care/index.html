Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance in Stillwater, MN

Commercial floor care for Stillwater offices and facilities — VCT, LVT, carpet, polished concrete.

Floor care in Stillwater

Commercial floor care in Stillwater follows the same pattern as the broader East Metro: Minnesota winter conditions accelerate finish degradation, and facilities that don't address salt residue and moisture tracking in November are dealing with a much bigger problem by March.

A typical Stillwater floor care scenario

A Stillwater Washington County courthouse-adjacent professional office in a 1920s commercial building on Main Street — original oak floors in the private office areas, ceramic tile in the corridor and restrooms, and a marble-tile entry vestibule. The oak floors need screening and recoating; the previous coating is worn through at doorways. The marble entry has etching from road-salt residue. A preservation-aware floor care program is needed — power-sanding or stripping would damage the original surfaces.

Services available in Stillwater

  • VCT strip and wax, scrub and recoat, burnishing
  • LVT/LVP scrub and recoat, low-speed maintenance
  • Polished concrete guard application and diamond-pad maintenance
  • Carpet hot-water extraction and interim encapsulation
  • Ceramic and porcelain tile scrub, grout cleaning, and sealing
  • Hardwood screening and recoating (surface work, not sanding)
