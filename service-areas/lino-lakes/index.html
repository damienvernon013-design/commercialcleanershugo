Commercial cleaning in Lino Lakes

Lino Lakes is part of our core service area. The drive from Hugo is short enough that we staff it on regular routes, not as an occasional extension. Businesses here get the same scope-document process and next-day response time as Hugo accounts.

  • Lino Lakes has experienced significant commercial development along Hodgson Road and the Century Avenue corridor, with newer office-warehouse buildings that have polished concrete floors rather than the VCT common in older suburban stock.
  • The city's location on I-35W makes it a distribution and light-industrial node — facilities in the Lino Lakes business parks often run second and third shifts, which affects cleaning scheduling and access windows.
  • Lino Lakes borders both Ramsey and Anoka Counties, and commercial property along County Road J and County Road H reflects a mix of county-boundary zoning that produced some unusual facility types, including several secured storage facilities with cleaning requirements.
