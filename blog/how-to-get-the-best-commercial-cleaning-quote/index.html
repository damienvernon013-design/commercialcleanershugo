How to Get the Best Commercial Cleaning Quote Without Overpaying

The businesses that pay fair prices for excellent service share a few habits before the cleaning company ever walks through the door.

Know your square footage

This sounds obvious, but many businesses give rough estimates. Cleaners price by the foot — the more accurate your number, the more accurate and fair your quote. Walk the space, measure common areas, and know which spaces are included vs. excluded (storage rooms, server rooms, etc.).

Define your scope before the walkthrough

Before inviting providers in for a quote, write down exactly what you need: which rooms, which tasks, how often, and what the non-negotiables are. This prevents providers from quoting a stripped-down service and prevents you from accepting something that won't actually keep your space clean.

Get more than one quote

One quote gives you a number. Several quotes give you a market. Middle-range quotes are usually the sweet spot — the cheapest bids often involve underqualified staff or hidden fees, while the most expensive aren't necessarily better.

Ask what's not included

Always ask providers to tell you what falls outside their standard quote. This surfaces hidden costs before you sign anything, not after.

Request references from similar businesses

A cleaning company that excels at retail spaces may not be the right fit for a medical office. Ask for references from businesses with similar size and type to yours.

Negotiate strategically

  • Offer a longer contract in exchange for a locked-in rate
  • Ask if paying quarterly reduces the monthly cost
  • Bundle services (floor care, restroom supplies) for a package rate
  • Compare quotes on the same day — quotes are valid for limited windows

Every quote from Hugo Commercial Cleaners starts with a facility walkthrough and comes with a written scope of work — no verbal-only estimates.
