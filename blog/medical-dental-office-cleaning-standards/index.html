Commercial Cleaning Standards for Medical & Dental Offices

Cleaning a medical or dental office is a fundamentally different task from cleaning a standard commercial office.

Why standard commercial cleaning isn't enough

General commercial cleaners are trained for appearance and basic hygiene. Medical environments require a higher level of disinfection — not just cleaning. The difference lies in the products used, the contact time required for disinfectants to work, and staff familiarity with handling surfaces that may be exposed to bodily fluids.

What medical cleaning includes that standard cleaning doesn't

  • Hospital-grade disinfectants used with appropriate contact time
  • Awareness of biohazard waste handling protocols
  • A clear distinction between clean and dirty zones within the facility
  • Frequent disinfection of all patient-contact surfaces between appointments
  • Use of color-coded microfiber cloths to prevent cross-contamination between zones

Patient privacy and access

Cleaning staff working in a medical office need protocols for staying out of patient records areas and maintaining confidentiality, even without directly handling any documents.

Questions to ask a medical cleaning provider

  • Is your staff trained on handling surfaces that may be exposed to bodily fluids?
  • Which disinfectants do you use, and can you provide the product information?
  • Do you have experience cleaning medical or dental offices specifically?
  • Can you provide documentation of your cleaning protocols for our records?
  • Are your staff background-checked?
