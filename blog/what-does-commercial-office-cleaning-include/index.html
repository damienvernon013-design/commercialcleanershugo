What Does a Commercial Office Cleaning Service Actually Include?

What's included in your quote varies widely between providers. Know exactly what you're buying before you sign.

Standard inclusions in most commercial cleaning contracts

  • Vacuuming: All carpeted areas, including under desks and in corners
  • Hard floor mopping: Tile, vinyl, hardwood — swept and mopped
  • Trash removal: All bins emptied and relined with new bags
  • Restroom cleaning: Toilets, sinks, mirrors, and floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked
  • Kitchen or breakroom: Counters, sink, and microwave exterior
  • Surface dusting: Desks, shelves, window ledges, and common surfaces
  • Glass and mirrors: Interior glass and glass partitions
  • Lobby and reception: Chairs, tables, and floors cleaned

Common add-ons (usually priced separately)

  • Carpet steam cleaning or extraction
  • Floor stripping, waxing, and buffing
  • Exterior window washing
  • High dusting (ceiling fans, vents, top of shelving)
  • Post-construction cleanup
  • Supply restocking (paper towels, hand soap, toilet paper) as a consumables package

What to ask your provider

When reviewing a quote, ask the provider to walk you through every task they will and won't perform. Request a written scope-of-work document rather than a verbal agreement — that's how every Hugo Commercial Cleaners quote is documented.

Customization is standard

Good commercial cleaning companies tailor their services to your space. If you have a server room that should never be entered or a kitchen with specific equipment, tell your provider. A well-structured contract should reflect your actual needs.
