Top 10 Areas Most Businesses Miss During Office Cleaning

Even offices that look clean often harbor neglected spots that build up grime over time.

The 10 most commonly missed areas

1. Keyboard and mouse surfaces

Keyboards accumulate far more bacteria than most people expect, yet most cleaning crews skip them entirely without explicit instructions. They should be disinfected at least weekly.

2. Light switches and outlet plates

High-touch surfaces that almost never get wiped down in standard cleaning routines.

3. Door handles and push plates

Touched hundreds of times daily, these are among the highest-transfer points in any commercial building.

4. Tops of filing cabinets and tall shelving

Out of sight, out of mind, until a client reaches up for something. High dusting requires extension tools and deliberate effort.

5. Refrigerator handles and microwave buttons

Kitchen appliances see constant use but are often wiped only on the exterior surfaces.

6. Air vents and return grilles

Dusty vents blow allergens and contaminants directly into breathing zones. These should be wiped monthly.

7. Chair bases and casters

Rolling chair wheels pick up debris from every floor surface and redistribute it.

8. Phone handsets

Used by mouth and ear multiple times per day, office phones are a significant germ vector that rarely appears on cleaning checklists.

9. Window blinds and tracks

Blinds accumulate dust rapidly and release it back into the air whenever they're opened or adjusted.

10. Under and behind furniture

Dust and debris accumulate under desks and behind furniture. Without periodic furniture-moving during deep cleans, these spots go unaddressed for months.
