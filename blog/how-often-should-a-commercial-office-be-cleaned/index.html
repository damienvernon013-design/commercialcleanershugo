How Often Should a Commercial Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

The right frequency depends on a few factors — get it wrong and you either overpay or undercleaning.

The general rule of thumb

For most office environments, 2–3 cleaning visits per week strikes the right balance between hygiene, cost, and necessity. High-traffic offices with more than 20 employees or client-facing spaces typically need daily service.

Factors that determine frequency

  • Number of employees: More people means more mess, more high-touch surfaces, more restroom usage.
  • Client traffic: If clients or customers visit your space regularly, cleanliness is tied to your brand. Daily service is usually worth it.
  • Facility type: Medical and dental offices require daily or even multiple-daily cleaning by industry standards.
  • Layout: Open-plan offices with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices with fewer shared touchpoints.
  • Season: Cold and flu season may warrant temporarily increasing frequency, and Hugo's long road-salt season (roughly October through mid-April) increases floor-care demands regardless of headcount.

Frequency by business type

  • Small professional office (under 10 people): 1–2x per week
  • Mid-size office (10–30 people): 2–3x per week
  • Large open-plan office (30+): Daily
  • Medical or dental office: Daily, plus routine sanitization protocols
  • Retail or warehouse: Daily to 3x per week depending on foot traffic

Don't forget periodic deep cleans

Regardless of your regular cleaning frequency, schedule periodic deep cleans for carpets and floor waxing. We offer these as add-ons at favorable rates for existing clients.
